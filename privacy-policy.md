# Privacy Policy — Reliable Download Manager

**Last updated:** 16 November 2026

Reliable Download Manager is developed by Prof. Dattaraj S. Vidyasagar & Prof. Yash D. Vidyasagar, for Vidyasagar Academy. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: **it does not collect, store or send any personal information about you.**

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect personal information, usage statistics, analytics or crash reports.
- It has no account, no sign-in and no advertising.
- It does not send anything about you or your computer to us or to anyone else.

## Network connections

The app connects to the internet only to do what you ask:

- **Downloads:** it connects to the server of each file you choose to download, exactly like a web browser does.
- **Grabber:** it reads only the web pages whose address you type into the Grabber, to find the files linked from them.

These servers may see your IP address and the request, as they would for any browser. The app sends them nothing else.

## Clipboard

To catch download links you copy, the app reads the text on your clipboard **on your computer only**. Nothing from the clipboard is stored or sent anywhere. You can switch this off in **Options > General**.

## Information kept on your computer

The app saves these files in its own folder on your computer, and nowhere else:

- the list of your downloads and their progress (so they can resume after a restart),
- your settings, queues and Grabber projects.

You can delete them at any time by uninstalling the app or removing its data folder.

## Links you choose to open

**Visit our website**, **Email** and **Tell a Friend** open your own browser, email program or WhatsApp only when you click them. What you then send is handled by those programs, not by Reliable Download Manager.

## Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
